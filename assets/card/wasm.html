Interactive demo

2×

Tab or arrows to move through fields and controls. Enter or a click copies a field or activates a control. Tap the moving border train to stop or resume it; drag a stopped train along the border. Hold minus or plus to change speed temporarily; release to restore it. Shift+Left or Right sets train speed from 0.5 to 6 times, starting at 2. Space pauses other motion. Page Up or Down scrolls short terminals. Esc pauses the demo.
