Interactive demo

Click or tap fields to copy · Focus app for Tab / arrow controls · Enter to activate · Shift+Left/Right: train 2× (0.5–6×)

Tab or arrows to move through fields and controls. Enter or a click copies a field or activates a control. Home selects Name, End selects Email. Shift+Left or Right adjusts train speed from 0.5 to 6 times, starting at 2. Space pauses motion. Page Up or Down scrolls short terminals. Esc pauses the demo.
