  • Playback control
    • Recap:
    • Run: 
      mpv --start=-56, --start=-00:56 # end time - 56 seconds. As negative indexing. 
mpv --start=50% # middle of the file.
mpv --start=30 --end=40 # at 30 seconds, plays 10 seconds, and exits.
mpv --start=-3:20 --length=10 # at 3:20 seconds before the end, plays 10s, and exits.
mpv --start='#2' --end='#4' # Plays chapters 2 and 3, and exits.
    • Resolution:
      • file/playlist loop: --loop-file=<N|inf|no>, --loop=<N|inf|no>
      • AB Loop set from cli: --ab-loop-a=<time>, --ab-loop-b=<time>
        • #default #keybinding l also do ab-loop, if you want to time the loop manually.
      • chapter from external metadata: --chapters-file=<filename>
