  • Other than measure-command or hyperfine, we can measure time within powershell easily.
    • Recap: with GetTimeStamp(),
      • high-resolution timestamp in ticks (100 nanoseconds per tick). Multiply by 100 to convert to nanoseconds.
    • Run: 
      $time = [System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch]::GetTimestamp();
Invoke-SudoPwsh "netstat -anb | sls :80";
[System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch]::GetTimestamp() - $time
    • Resolution: this is the most convenient way to benchmark a command.
