      • Broad phase and narrow phase in Collision Detection
        • Broad phase: the physics engine will determine which objects can possibly collide against each other, and then store the potential object collision in a list- this is often known as a collision pair.
          • Narrow phase: the engine iterates over the list of potential collision pairs, and determines whether they really are colliding, and if so, resolves the collision.
