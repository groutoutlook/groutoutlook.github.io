      • #selection #placing Kicad pcbnew Aligning objects
        • select multiple(minimum 2) objects(mainly footprints and pads), there are two paths:
          • Right Click -> Align/Distribute -> Align to Left/Right/Horizontal Center | Top/Bottom/Vertical Center
            • upon that menu, you can a a -> d d or you can bind hotkey and Align, havent bind one yet.
            • Normally you align by center, then by distance.
          • Reason: really useful when you align an array of transistors/via/line driver IC. Tracks, not so much.
            • or when you want to align text on silkscreen. That shit cost lots of time.
        • Final selection is the anchor, be wise on select it, you can reselect to your like.
    • Revisit: Due to the Align/Distribute #til you can use CUI-like navigation(pressing initial) to move to wanted functionality. Another tip for (potentially) scripting UI (using ahk or pals).
