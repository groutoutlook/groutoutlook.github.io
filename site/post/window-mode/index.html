    • Resolution:
      • Ctrl+w ctrl+s split Horizontal bottom, of same files.
        • To be honest this is the most convenient one.
      • Ctrl+w ctrl+t transpose the direct child and parent split.
        • e.g. from horizontal to vertical and vice versa.
      • Ctrl+w ctrl+o is exotic version, close all other panes.
