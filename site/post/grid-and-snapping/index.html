  • Grid and snapping
    • Recap: shift+s toggle between snapping on current layer or all layer.
      • e.g. cant snap top layer with bottom objects after you choose current layer snapping mode.
    • Run: especially useful while routing.
    • Resolution: Holding ctrl will disable grid snapping.
      • So instead of changing to super small grid, just hold ctrl while moving items.
      • Holding shift will disable object snapping. Same will apply when you route x, super useful.
      • Hold both ctrl and shift is possible.
