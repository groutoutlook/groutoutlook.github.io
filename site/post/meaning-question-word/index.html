MEANING QUESTION WORD
people (Who?) 谁 shéi /shei
things (What?) 什么 shénme /shenme
specific things (Which?) 哪 nǎ which/na
time (When?) 什么时候 shénme shíhòu
places (Where?) 哪儿 nǎ’er, 哪里 nǎ lǐ /nali
character, process, style (How? How is it?) 怎么 zenme
quantity or number (How much?) 几 jǐ，多少 duoshao
