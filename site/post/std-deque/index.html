`std::deque`

2025-07-18 09:26:37 AM
  • container/queue
  • std::deque
    • Recap: use deque when you access through push_front/push_back/pop_front/pop_back.
      • or std::deque<T,Allocator>::emplace_back
      • Recite: std::deque is used to avoid the cost of reallocating when a std::vector would grow, and typically a recorder is used in cases when the length is unknown.
        • Root: thru glz::recorder. I do think I need this one on my chip.
    • Run:
    • Resolution:
      • std::deque<T,Allocator>::insert
        • Calling insert in the middle of a std::deque invalidates all iterators and references to its elements.
          • After insert you have to reassign every iterator or make all of them RAII.
      • std::deque<T,Allocator>::append_range
        • this is not so unique, apparently every contigiuos container has this.
          • See C++ named requirements: ContiguousContainer (since C++17)
      • unique to deque, we have std::deque<T,Allocator>::prepend_range
    • Reason:
    • Root: std::queue
    • Related:
      • emplace_back/emplace_front dont move, it constructs the element on the spot. push_back/push_front move things which cost some CPU cycles.
