A soap opera, daytime drama, or soap for short, is typically a long-running radio or film series, frequently characterized by melodrama, ensemble casts, and sentimentality.[1] The term "soap opera" originated from radio dramas originally being sponsored by soap manufacturers.[2] The term was preceded by "horse opera", a derogatory term for low-budget Westerns.
