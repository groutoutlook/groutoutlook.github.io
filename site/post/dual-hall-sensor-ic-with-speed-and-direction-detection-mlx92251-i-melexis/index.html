  • Dual Hall Sensor IC with Speed and Direction Detection (MLX92251) I Melexis
    • Recap: double Hall sensor for speed and direction measure.
      • Two Hall plates are integrated on the same piece of silicon, thus using the high precision of the wafer fabrication process to ensure a fixed spacing of 1.45 mm between the sensing elements. The first Hall plate signal is used to provide the speed signal output. The combination of both the first and second Hall plate signals is then internally processed to directly deliver a direction signal output.
