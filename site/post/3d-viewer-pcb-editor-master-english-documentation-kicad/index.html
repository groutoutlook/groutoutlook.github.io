  • 3D Viewer | PCB Editor | master | English | Documentation | KiCad
    • Recap: scroll action to zoom in / zoom out.
    • Run: Alt+3 open 3D viewer, In that we can pinch/spread to move view port horizontal
      • Pan camera by slide two finger Horizontally/vertically.
      • d toggle DNP: Do not populate component model in case you want to look at copper layer underneath
      • home to the original viewport (x+/y+/z+). Undo every kind of rotation as well. Z+ is from screen origin to our eyes.
      • x/y/z accordingly spin back to that axis.
      • r/shift+r rotate on z-axis.
      • you can toggle Appearance tab in "View" -> "Show Appearance Manager" (alt+v s)
      • Space Set Pivot point around which the board will be rotated (middle mouse click). UI will show animation of clicking on that point you chose.
