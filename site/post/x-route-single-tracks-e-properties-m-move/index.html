  • x route single tracks, e properties, m move.
    • Recap: w e edit track custom width regardless of default.
      • a will select all while popped up.
      • while clicking, 1,2 to choose. Hold Shift choose extra.
    • Run:
      • In route mode, v spawn via and continue route in the bottom layer, Enter to confirm via position.
        • but v in normal mode alternate between b.Cu and f.Cu when you are not actively routing
        • ctrl+e route from the other end, if you have multiple ends then it will pick the one you hover.
    • Resolution: when select you better choose layers first. Too many things are elide on the screen...
      • especially true if you have too many interconnect between layers.
      • Quickest way to get dimension of PCB is select Edge.Cuts and e.
      • while routing and want to pin/static the track direction, Enter quicker than double click.
        • same apply with Measure Tool/Ruler. static the ruler by Enter. Better than double click.
    • Reason:
      • I kept mistaking tracks with vias...
